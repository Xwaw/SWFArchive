# About Me

## Hey there!

Hello! I'm **Xwaw**, an independent developer, software engineering student, and the creator of **SWFArchive**.

I've always been passionate about video games, programming, and technology. Over the years, I've worked on various personal projects, experimenting with different programming languages, game engines, and web technologies.

But there's one thing that has always had a special place in my heart — **Flash games and the internet of the 2000s**.

## My Journey Into Programming

My interest in programming started with video games. I was fascinated by how games worked behind the scenes and wanted to understand how to create my own.

Over time, I began experimenting with different technologies, learning how to build games, applications, and websites.

Some of the technologies I've worked with include:

- **C#** — Game development and backend applications.
- **JavaScript & TypeScript** — Interactive websites and web applications.
- **React** — Building modern user interfaces.
- **HTML & CSS** — Website design and styling.
- **Unity** — Developing games and experimenting with gameplay mechanics.
- **Lua** — Scripting and creating game-related systems.

Programming has become more than just a hobby for me. It's something I want to keep improving at and eventually turn into a long-term career.

## Why Flash Games?

Back in the day, Flash games were everywhere.

You didn't need an expensive computer, a gaming console, or even an account. All you needed was a browser, an internet connection, and a little free time.

Websites like **Newgrounds, Kongregate, Miniclip, and Armor Games** offered thousands of games created by developers from all around the world.

Some were simple, some were incredibly creative, and others were surprisingly challenging.

What made Flash special wasn't just the games themselves, but the atmosphere surrounding them.

The colorful websites, animated buttons, unusual designs, and communities made the internet feel like a completely different world.

And honestly, I miss that era.

## Why I Created SWFArchive

SWFArchive started as a personal project combining two things I enjoy: **web development and classic Flash games**.

I wanted to create something inspired by the websites of the late 2000s and early 2010s, while still using modern technologies behind the scenes.

My goal isn't simply to build another game library.

I want SWFArchive to feel like a place from the old internet — where you can discover forgotten games, explore interesting content, customize your profile, and interact with other players.

It's also an opportunity for me to improve my programming skills and experiment with new ideas.

## Behind the Scenes

SWFArchive is an independent project developed and maintained by me.

The website is built using technologies such as **React, TypeScript, ASP.NET Core, and SQLite**.

I'm responsible for its design, frontend, backend, and overall development.

Since I'm working on the project in my free time, updates and new features may take some time.

There's still a lot I want to improve, and the project will continue to evolve as I gain more experience.
