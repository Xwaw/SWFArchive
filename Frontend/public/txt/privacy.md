# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: October 19, 2026**

Welcome to **SWFArchive**!

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information SWFArchive collects, how it is used, and how we protect your personal data.

By using SWFArchive, you acknowledge that you have been informed about the data processing practices described in this policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

When you use SWFArchive, we may collect the following information:

### Account Information

- Username
- Email address
- Securely stored password hash
- Account creation date
- Account roles and permissions

### Profile Information

Depending on the features you use, your profile may contain:

- Profile picture and banner
- Profile description and status
- Friends list
- Favorite games
- Badges and achievements

Some of this information may be visible to other users.

### Communication Data

SWFArchive provides social features such as private messages and friend requests.

Messages and related information may be stored to provide these services.

Private messages are not intended to be publicly visible. However, authorized access may be necessary for security, abuse investigations, or legal compliance.

### Game-Related Information

We may process information associated with:

- Uploaded games
- Game submissions and moderation
- Favorite games
- Game activity, where tracking features are enabled

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We process personal information to:

- Create and maintain user accounts.
- Provide website functionality.
- Enable communication between users.
- Manage uploaded content.
- Maintain website security.
- Prevent spam, abuse, and unauthorized access.
- Respond to user requests.
- Improve website performance and reliability.

The applicable legal basis for each processing purpose, such as performance of a contract, legitimate interests, consent, or legal obligations, will be specified in the completed policy.

## 3. Passwords and Account Security

SWFArchive is designed to use secure authentication mechanisms.

Passwords should be stored using cryptographic password hashing rather than as readable text.

Users are responsible for keeping their login credentials confidential.

If you believe your account has been compromised, please contact the website administrator.

## 4. Cookies and Local Storage

SWFArchive may use cookies or browser storage for:

- Authentication and session management.
- Remembering user preferences.
- Maintaining essential website functionality.

Any non-essential tracking or analytics technologies will be identified separately, along with applicable consent requirements.

## 5. Sharing Your Information

SWFArchive does not intend to sell users' personal information.

Certain information may be shared with service providers where necessary to operate the website, such as hosting or infrastructure providers.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law.

Details of actual recipients and international data transfers will be provided where applicable.

## 6. Third-Party Content and Flash Games

SWFArchive provides access to Flash games and may use emulation technologies such as **Ruffle**.

Some games may contain external links or resources.

Third-party websites and services may have their own privacy policies.

SWFArchive does not control the privacy practices of independent third-party websites.

## 7. Data Retention

Personal information is retained only for as long as necessary for the purposes for which it was collected or as required by applicable law.

The final policy will specify retention periods or the criteria used to determine them.

## 8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable data protection laws, including the GDPR, you may have the right to:

- Access your personal data.
- Correct inaccurate personal information.
- Request deletion of your personal data.
- Restrict certain processing activities.
- Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
- Request data portability where applicable.
- Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
- Lodge a complaint with a competent data protection authority.

In Poland, the relevant supervisory authority is the **President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO)**.

## 9. Account Deletion

Users may request deletion of their accounts and associated personal information.

Some information may need to be retained where required by law or justified by another applicable legal basis.

Account deletion procedures and contact details will be specified when this functionality is available.

## 10. Children's Privacy

SWFArchive is committed to protecting younger users.

Age eligibility requirements and any necessary parental consent procedures will be established before the service is publicly launched.
